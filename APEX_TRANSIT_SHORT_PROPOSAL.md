## Resale Value (How It's Set)

The resale figure is not an estimate — it is calculated using a three-source methodology
before the investment agreement is signed. The final resale figure determines the
monthly payment, so it is verified and agreed upfront.

- **Three SA sources checked:** AutoTrader, Cars.co.za, and a WeBuyCars / dealer
  trade-in offer — all at the vehicle's projected end-of-term kilometre reading.
- **End-of-term km** is calculated as: starting mileage + (4,809 km/month × 36) =
  starting mileage + ~173,124 km.
- **Conservative figure used:** the lower of the trade-in offer or the 25th-percentile
  of advertised prices — protecting the investor against optimistic assumptions.
- **Finalised only once a specific vehicle is selected** — until then, a range is used
  (R60,000–R120,000 at R250,000 purchase, depending on model and condition).
- **If the vehicle sells for more than the agreed figure, the investor keeps 100% of the
  actual proceeds.**
