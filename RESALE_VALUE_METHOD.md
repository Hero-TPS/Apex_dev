# Resale Value Methodology — Apex Transit

**Purpose:** This document explains, in plain language, how Apex Transit will determine
a defensible resale estimate before finalising any investor agreement. This figure
directly affects the monthly investor payment and must not be a guess.

---

## Why Resale Value Matters

The investment formula is:

> **Monthly payment = (Investment × 1.10 − Resale proceeds) ÷ 36**

A lower resale estimate means a higher monthly payment for the operator. A higher
resale estimate means less is paid monthly — but if the actual resale at month 36 falls
short, the investor is underpaid. The methodology below protects both parties.

---

## Step 1 — Three-Source Market Check (South African Sources)

Once a specific vehicle (make / model / trim / year) has been identified, a price check
is performed across three South African sources for the **same make, model, trim, and
approximate kilometre reading** as the vehicle will have at month 36:

| Source | Method |
|---|---|
| **AutoTrader** (autotrader.co.za) | Search the exact model at the projected end-of-term km. Note the lowest 25% of listed prices. |
| **Cars.co.za** | Repeat the same search. Compare spread of listed prices. |
| **WeBuyCars / dealer trade-in offer** | Get an actual offer or online estimate on the vehicle at the projected km. This is the floor — it is what a dealer will pay today for a similar vehicle at that mileage. |

> All three checks are done at the **projected end-of-term kilometre reading**, not at
> the vehicle's current mileage. See Step 2 for how that number is calculated.

---

## Step 2 — Projecting End-of-Term Kilometres

| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| Average monthly km | **4,809 km/month** (3-month verified average, Dec 2025 – Feb 2026) |
| Term | **36 months** |
| km added over term | 4,809 × 36 = **173,124 km** |
| Starting mileage | Varies by vehicle — recorded at time of purchase |
| **Projected end-of-term km** | **Starting mileage + 173,124 km** |

**Examples:**

| Starting Mileage | End-of-Term km |
|---|---|
| 10,000 km | ~183,000 km |
| 30,000 km | ~203,000 km |
| 50,000 km | ~223,000 km |

> The vehicle requirement is a maximum of 50,000 km at purchase. Resale estimates must
> reflect the correct end-of-term mileage for the specific vehicle, not a round estimate.

---

## Step 3 — Conservative Policy (Investor Protection)

From the three-source check, two reference values are extracted:

- **Trade-in floor** — the WeBuyCars / dealer offer (cash offer, no negotiation)
- **25th-percentile advertised price** — the lower quarter of AutoTrader + Cars.co.za listings

> **The resale figure used in the investment agreement is the lower of these two values.**

This is deliberately conservative. It protects the investor against optimistic estimates.
If the vehicle sells for more, the investor receives the full proceeds — any upside
goes entirely to the investor.

---

## Step 4 — When the Resale Figure Is Finalised

| Stage | Resale Treatment |
|---|---|
| Before a specific vehicle is selected | **Ranges only** — see sensitivity table below |
| Once vehicle is identified but not yet purchased | Three-source check performed; range narrows |
| Final figure agreed with investor | Locked into the written agreement before purchase |

> **No single resale number is guaranteed until a specific vehicle is selected and the
> three-source check has been completed.**

## Sensitivity Table — Investment R250,000 at 10% Return

This table shows how the monthly investor payment changes depending on the resale
outcome. All scenarios use the same formula: `(R275,000 − Resale) ÷ 36`.

| Resale Value | Monthly Payment | Monthly Saving vs Rental (R11,267) | Running Costs | Net Monthly Position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R120,000 (optimistic) | R4,306 | +R6,961 before costs | R4,253 | **+R2,708** |
| R100,000 | R4,861 | +R6,406 before costs | R4,253 | **+R2,153** |
| R80,000 | R5,417 | +R5,850 before costs | R4,253 | **+R1,597** |
| R60,000 (conservative) | R5,972 | +R5,295 before costs | R4,253 | **+R1,042** |

> Running costs: R2,100 insurance + R1,603 service provision + R400 tyres/wear + R150 licensing.
>
> **At every scenario above, the deal remains cash-flow positive vs current rental.**
> The deal does not break even unless resale falls below approximately R30,000 —
> which would require a vehicle in non-roadworthy condition.

---

## What Goes Into the Investor Agreement

Once a vehicle is selected, the following resale-related items are included in the
written agreement:

1. The specific vehicle (make, model, trim, year, VIN once purchased)
2. The three-source check results (printed or screenshot, dated)
3. The agreed resale figure used for the monthly payment calculation
4. A clause that **100% of actual sale proceeds go to the investor** at month 36
5. A clause that if actual proceeds exceed the agreed resale, the investor keeps the difference

## Summary

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where does the resale figure come from? | Three SA sources: AutoTrader, Cars.co.za, WeBuyCars |
| What km is used for the check? | Starting mileage + 173,124 km (4,809/month × 36) |
| Which figure is used? | The lower of: trade-in offer or 25th-percentile advertised price |
| When is it finalised? | Only once a specific vehicle is selected |
| What if it sells for more? | Investor keeps 100% of proceeds |
