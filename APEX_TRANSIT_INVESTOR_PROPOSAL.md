# APEX TRANSIT
## Vehicle Investment Proposal
**March 2026 | Confidential**

---

## The Business

Apex Transit is an established private transport business operating in South Africa,
providing scheduled passenger transport (private bookings) and ride-hailing services
(Uber). The business has been operational and generating consistent monthly revenue,
supported by a full digital management system tracking all income, expenses, trips,
and fuel.

---

## The Opportunity

Apex Transit currently operates using a **rented vehicle at R2,600/week
(±R11,267/month)**. We are seeking an investor to fund the purchase of an owned
vehicle, replacing the rental entirely. This eliminates a significant recurring cost,
improves monthly profitability, and provides the investor with a **fixed monthly
return over 36 months**.
